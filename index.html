01 · The problem

The network effect is real. Your systems don't show it yet.

Leadership has described a platform where every partner brings a "superpower" the others can learn from. Today that sharing happens on monthly calls and in playbooks rolled out one partner at a time. Below are six places where it stalls. Click any card to see the fix.

Pain 01 · Demand after dark

50,000 calls a year come in after the office closes

Leadership has said these are calls that would normally go missed. H2OCP is now big enough to build infrastructure to answer them, and the choice of stack will decide whether that becomes a cost center or a booking engine.

Agentforce Voice + Service Cloud

Every call answered, qualified, and booked

  1. A voice agent answers in each local brand's name, 24/7, with that brand's hours, service area, and offers
  2. It qualifies the caller (city or well water, symptoms, equipment, urgency) and books a water test or service visit
  3. It writes the job and transcript back to the schedule, so the team starts the morning with booked work instead of voicemails
Board impact: after-hours demand turns into booked revenue with no extra headcount.
Pain 02 · Speed-to-lead

34 front doors, each responding at its own speed

Marketing generates leads centrally, but each brand works them locally. Leadership has said it is testing AI for speed-to-lead. The next step is making the winning test the default across all 34 brands at once.

Sales Cloud + Agentforce SDR

A web form gets a response in minutes

  1. Every form, call, and chat from every brand lands in one queue, scored and routed to the right local team
  2. An agent texts back right away, answers basic questions, and offers water-test time slots
  3. Conversion is measured by brand, channel, and rep, so the best playbook can be rolled out to every brand
Board impact: more organic growth from each partner, with no added acquisition spend.

Data 360 + Marketing Cloud

One customer view, and playbooks that run themselves

  1. Data 360 combines customer, equipment, and service history from every brand's field system into one profile
  2. Triggered journeys go out for filter changes, salt delivery, re-tests, and reviews, in each brand's own voice
  3. A new partner inherits every proven journey on day one instead of waiting months for a rollout
Board impact: more recurring, predictable service revenue, which is what buyers pay a premium for.

02 · The platform

A network layer for the whole platform

Salesforce doesn't replace the local brands, the local teams, or what makes each partner special. It adds the one thing a platform of 34 brands can't get from 34 separate systems: one shared view of every customer, lead, and partner.

Agentforce Voice & Service Cloud

A 24/7 voice agent answers in each brand's name, qualifies callers, and books visits. Agents escalate to on-call staff when needed.

Replaces: missed calls, voicemail, and next-morning callbacks

Sales Cloud

Two pipelines on one platform: homeowner leads for all 34 brands, and partner development for the M&A team.

Replaces: per-brand lead handling and deal-tracking spreadsheets

Marketing Cloud

Branded, triggered journeys for filter changes, salt, re-tests, reviews, and win-backs, across email, SMS, and direct mail.

Replaces: playbooks rolled out one partner at a time

Data 360

Combines customer, equipment, and service history from every brand's field system into one profile, which powers the agents and journeys.

Replaces: 30+ disconnected customer databases

Tableau Next

A network scorecard with AI-flagged outliers, from each brand up to the region, the network, and the board.

Replaces: hand-built roll-ups and board decks

Slack

Channels per deal, a leader-council workspace, and agent alerts where the team already works. The network learns in real time, not just once a month.

Replaces: email chains and once-a-month knowledge sharing

Contentful · Palmata

Measure and improve how answer engines describe every brand, and publish content to 30+ brand sites from one place.

Adds: visibility into AI answers, alongside the SEO program

    05 · Agentforce

    Four agents doing specific jobs for H2OCP

    These aren't generic chatbots. Each agent has a clear trigger, a decision to make, and an action to take within H2OCP's operating model, and each one hands off to a person whenever it should.

    After-Hours Voice Agent

    Answers for all 34 brands, 24/7
    Trigger
    An inbound call outside business hours, or overflow during peak times
    Decides
    Whether it's an emergency (leak or no water) needing on-call escalation, a new lead, or an existing customer needing service
    Acts
    Books a water test or service visit, texts a confirmation, writes the record back, and pages on-call staff if urgent

    Speed-to-Lead SDR

    Works every form, chat, and missed call
    Trigger
    A new web form, chat, or missed call on any brand
    Decides
    Lead fit (service area, water source, owner vs. renter) and the best next step
    Acts
    Texts within minutes, answers FAQs, offers test slots, and routes hot leads to a local rep

    Service Renewal Agent

    Keeps the installed base on schedule
    Trigger
    A filter, salt, or re-test due date from equipment and install data in Data 360
    Decides
    Channel, timing, and offer based on customer history and brand rules
    Acts
    Sends the branded reminder, handles replies, books the visit, and asks for a review afterward

    Partner Onboarding Agent

    Supports the resource team from signed deal to first 90 days
    Trigger
    A partnership moves to Closed in the partner-development pipeline
    Decides
    Which integration playbook applies (finance, people, field system, marketing) and who owns each step
    Acts
    Opens the Slack channel, assigns tasks, tracks data migration, and flags blockers to the Chief Transformation Office

    08 · Roadmap

    Three phases, each unlocked by the one before it

    Timed to the growth plan leadership has described for the next twelve months (after-hours infrastructure, people investment, new locations), starting in the first year after the July 2026 recapitalization.

    Phase 1

    Answer every call

    • Agentforce Voice after-hours agent on 3–5 pilot brands
    • Integration with the existing field system for booking and write-back
    • Palmata AI-visibility baseline across all brand sites
    Milestone: pilot brands live, with every after-hours call answered and logged
    Unlocks Phase 2 when: booked-job rate on after-hours calls meets the agreed target
    Phase 2

    One customer view

    • Data 360 combines installed-base data across brands
    • Marketing Cloud renewal and review journeys on pilot brands
    • Sales Cloud lead routing and SDR agent; partner-development pipeline goes live
    Milestone: first cross-brand renewal journey live, and the M&A team runs its pipeline in Sales Cloud
    Unlocks Phase 3 when: measurable lift in renewal response and speed-to-lead on pilot brands
    Phase 3

    The network operating system

    • Roll out to all brands; new partners onboard onto the platform within 30 days
    • Tableau Next network scorecard for regional presidents, leader council, and board
    • Field Service consolidation assessment, done on H2OCP's terms
    Milestone: board-ready network scorecard, with every brand on one data foundation
    Result: faster organic growth per partner and faster time to value on every acquisition
